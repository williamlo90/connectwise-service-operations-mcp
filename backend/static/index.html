THE WORKFLOW

Clear ownership at every step.

  1. Prepare as an operator

    Choose a ticket and supply observations. Time entries require documented minutes and a start time.

  2. Review as another approver

    Switch account, sign in as approver-a or approver-b, and open the proposal. Approval is bound to the exact payload.

  3. Execute as the proposer

    Switch back to the original operator. The backend writes and checks the resulting record.

  4. Reconcile uncertainty

    If the result is unknown or needs review, verify the existing operation. Do not create a replacement write.
